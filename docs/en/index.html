Detailed Setup Guide

Follow these comprehensive steps to configure dynamic wallpapers on your device.

1

Download the Motion Wallpaper File

Visit the wallpaper library at mwallx.com on your computer or device. Browse the collection and download the .tendies motion wallpaper format file of your choice.

2

Get the Nugget Tool

Download and open the latest release of Nugget from the official Nugget GitHub repository. You will need a macOS or Windows computer to run this desktop utility.

3

Import and Inject settings

Connect your iPhone or iPad to your computer. In Nugget, browse to import your downloaded .tendies file. Follow Nugget instructions to inject and apply the wallpaper configuration payload to your device.

4

Activate Live Wallpaper

On your iOS / iPadOS device, navigate to Settings -> Wallpaper. Select the newly imported motion wallpaper and enable the live effect. Test lock screen long-press or home screen movements to verify the live animations.
